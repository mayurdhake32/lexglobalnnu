Lex Global Review of Law and Justice (LGRLJ)

“Justice · Scholarship · Integrity · Excellence” — An International Peer-Reviewed Law Journal

Introduced by Lex Global Advocacy LLP, Mumbai, the Lex Global Review of Law and Justice (LGRLJ) is a flagship scholarly periodical dedicated to critical doctrinal jurisprudence, transnational commercial arbitration, cross-border treaty frameworks, and contemporary global justice.

Peer-Reviewed Rigor Double-blind peer review by distinguished jurists, senior counsel, and international legal scholars.
Global Circulation Indexed across international legal repositories, institutional libraries, and open-access academic archives.
Courtroom & Policy Impact Bridging academic legal doctrine with practical dispute strategies and legislative policy reform.
Print & Digital Editions Available via institutional digital repository and archival print subscription distribution.
